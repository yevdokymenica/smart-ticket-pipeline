
# Complaints and Customer Service
Document ID: KB-004
Category: Beschwerde

IMPORTANT: Fictional demonstration policy.

## 1. Customer Complaints

When customers express dissatisfaction:

- Identify the main reason for the complaint.
- Record the relevant interaction history.
- Summarize the customer's concern.
- Maintain neutral and professional communication.

## 2. Delayed Processing

For complaints about processing delays:

- Identify the original request date.
- Check the latest available processing status.
- Document previous customer contacts.
- Forward unresolved delays to the responsible department.

## 3. Communication Problems

When customers report contradictory information:

- Record the conflicting statements.
- Identify the responsible communication channels.
- Request clarification from the responsible team.
- Document the resolution.

## 4. Escalation

Escalate serious or unresolved complaints to the designated complaint management team.
