
# General Customer Information
Document ID: KB-005
Category: Sonstiges

IMPORTANT: Fictional demonstration policy.

## 1. Membership Information

For general membership questions:

- Identify the customer's information request.
- Provide relevant general documentation.
- Refer account-specific questions to the appropriate support team.

## 2. Online Portal

For questions about the online customer portal:

- Explain the available registration information.
- Provide general navigation guidance.
- Refer technical problems to technical support.

## 3. Contact Information

For questions about service channels:

- Identify the requested contact information.
- Provide only information available in the knowledge base.
- Do not invent opening hours or contact details.

## 4. General Questions

When an inquiry does not belong to another category:

- Identify the main subject.
- Search the available documentation.
- Forward unresolved questions to the responsible department.
