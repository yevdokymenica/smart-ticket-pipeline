
# Insurance Benefits and Treatments
Document ID: KB-003
Category: Leistung

IMPORTANT: Fictional demonstration policy.

## 1. Treatment Coverage

When customers ask whether a treatment is covered:

- Identify the requested treatment.
- Collect the relevant service information.
- Check the applicable demonstration coverage documentation.
- Do not promise reimbursement without verification.

## 2. Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation

For physiotherapy and rehabilitation requests:

- Identify the type of treatment.
- Check whether prior approval is required under the applicable policy.
- Request relevant supporting documentation.
- Forward unclear cases for specialist review.

## 3. Preventive Healthcare

For questions about vaccinations, preventive examinations or health courses:

- Identify the requested service.
- Search the relevant coverage documentation.
- Explain which information is available.
- Escalate questions that require individual assessment.

## 4. Escalation

Forward cases requiring medical or coverage assessment to the appropriate specialist team.
